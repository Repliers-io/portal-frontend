## Unverified boundaries

The source in this checkout cannot establish the following; pages that touch them label the claim.

- **Repliers.** Search scoring and union ordering, the NLP model, the estimate model, similarity
  and history ordering, Messages delivery, quotas and rate limiting. Behaviour observed against
  the sample-data key on a given date is labelled with that date and may differ for other
  accounts.
- **Follow Up Boss.** Redelivery of failed webhook callbacks, how it reads `id[]` parameters,
  whether its own updates fire webhooks, tag replacement semantics.
- **NATS server.** Consumer defaults (acknowledgement policy and wait, maximum deliveries),
  redelivery limits, and client behaviour during a reconnect.
- **Redis.** Eviction, failover, and how long a call waits during an outage beyond the observed
  8 s.
- **Mapbox and Google Maps.** Ranking and response content.
- **Signals.** `SIGINT` and `SIGTERM` handling is read from the source; the probes ran on
  Windows, where a signal cannot be delivered to a Node process from outside.
- **Node version.** Probes ran on Node 26.10.0, not on the 24.x line that `package.json`
  requires.
- **Deployments.** Environments, presets, secrets, proxy and CDN chains, process counts and
  log handling of any real deployment are not inferred from this checkout.
- **The frontend.** What the Next.js SSR and SSG renders send is described from what the backend
  accepts; the frontend's code is not in this repository.
